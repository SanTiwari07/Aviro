# ARIVO Settlement & Waterfall Policy

- **Policy Name**: Gateway Settlement Waterfall & Discrepancy Policy
- **Policy Version**: 2.3.0
- **Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
- **Domain**: Settlement Accounting, Deductions, Bank Transfer (UTR), and Cash Timing
- **Status**: Authoritative

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy mandates the exact accounting arithmetic applied to all settlement batches received from acquiring partners, payment gateways, and banking networks. It governs deduction breakdowns and defines unexplained discrepancy thresholds.

---

## 2. Waterfall Formula

The Net Settlement amount deposited into the corporate operating bank account must mathematically satisfy:

$$\text{Net Amount} = \text{Gross Amount} - \text{Fees} - \text{Tax} - \text{Refunds} - \text{Chargebacks} + \text{Adjustments}$$

All calculations must be performed strictly using integer minor units (paise). Floating-point arithmetic is strictly prohibited.

### Components
1. **Gross Amount**: Total transaction value authorized and captured across payments grouped within this settlement cycle.
2. **Fees**: Contractual Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) and transaction convenience fees.
3. **Tax**: Goods and Services Tax (GST at 18%) applied exclusively to the fee component.
4. **Refunds**: Merchant-initiated customer refunds netted from current cycle gross receipts.
5. **Chargebacks**: Disputed transaction debits withheld by acquiring banks pending representment.
6. **Adjustments**: Pre-approved credits or debits issued by payment processors for prior cycle corrections.

---

## 3. Discrepancy & Anomaly Handling

### Rule SET-001: Strict Mathematical Balancing
- $\text{Expected Net} = \text{Gross} - \text{Fees} - \text{Tax} - \text{Refunds} - \text{Chargebacks} + \text{Adjustments}$.
- $\text{Unexplained Delta} = |\text{Expected Net} - \text{Actual Net}|$.
- If $\text{Unexplained Delta} > 0$, the settlement is categorized as `WATERFALL_ANOMALY`.
- The reconciliation case associated with a waterfall anomaly is automatically forced to `EXCEPTION` status. No automated match is permitted.

### Rule SET-002: Bank UTR Reconciliation
Every settlement batch has a Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) generated by the clearing bank (e.g., NEFT/RTGS/IMPS).
- The settlement amount must match the corresponding credit entry on the primary bank statement.
- Unmatched UTRs older than $T+2$ business days are escalated to Treasury Operations.

### Rule SET-003: Timing Lag & Cutoff Window
Gateways settle on a $T+1$ or $T+2$ business day lag cycle.
- Transactions captured prior to 23:59:59 IST are bundled into the designated batch.
- Weekend and public bank holiday settlements accumulate and clear on the subsequent business banking day.
